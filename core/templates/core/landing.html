Why Pill Pal
Everything your treatment needs, nothing it doesn't.
💊

Smart Reminders

Automated, personalised reminders at exactly the right time — via SMS, voice call, or push notification — in your language.

✅

Easy Confirmation

Confirm your dose with a single SMS reply, a missed call, or one tap on the app. No login required for SMS users.

👥

Caregiver Alerts

A trusted caregiver is quietly notified if you miss doses — so you're never alone on your treatment journey.

🏥

Clinic Dashboard

Healthcare workers see real-time adherence data and can identify at-risk patients before they fall off treatment.

📚

Medication Information

Plain-language drug information in your language — what it does, side effects, and what to do if you miss a dose.

🔒

Private & Secure

Your health data is encrypted and POPIA-compliant. Caregivers see adherence rates only — not your diagnosis.

How it works
Up and running in minutes.
1

Register

Create your account or ask your clinic to register you. Choose your preferred reminder channel.

2

Set your schedule

Your clinic configures your medication schedule. Reminders are automatically activated.

3

Receive reminders

Get reminders at the right time, via SMS, voice, or app — even without a smartphone.

4

Confirm & track

Reply to confirm your dose. Your adherence is logged and visible to you and your care team.

Multi-Channel Access
Works on every phone. No data required for SMS & Voice.
📱

SMS

Any phone, any network. Receive reminders and confirm doses by replying with a single number. Works with zero mobile data.

📞

Voice Call

An automated call in your language reads your reminder and lets you confirm by pressing 1 on your keypad.

📲

Mobile App

Smartphone users get a rich dashboard, visual adherence tracking, and one-tap confirmation from push notifications.
